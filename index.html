About Us

We like to pride ourselves on being a very open, fun, running group on campus. We are an inclusive group that
welcomes people from all backgrounds and all running levels. We are all about promoting health and well-being
and we also emphasize the importance of making friends.

We care not only about our running community but also the community around us. We have volunteered at the
Austin Marathon, supported local businesses with profit shares, and even partnered with an Austin-based shoe
store to give our members good deals and fun collabs!
